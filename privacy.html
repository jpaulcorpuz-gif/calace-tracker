Privacy Policy

CAL ACE Student Tracker · Last updated November 23, 2026

The CAL ACE Student Tracker is an internal tool used by staff of CAL ACE Nursing Academy to manage student enrollment, payments, requirements and documents. It is not offered to the public.

Who can use it

Only staff members whose email address is on the school's staff list can sign in. Everyone else is refused.

What information it holds

  • Student records entered by staff: names, contact details, enrollment, payments and requirement status.
  • Student documents uploaded by staff, such as Live Scan forms, signed 283B forms, physical exam and TB test results.
  • A log of each change, recording which staff member made it and when.

How Google Drive is used

When an administrator connects Google Drive, the tracker asks only for permission to work with files it creates (drive.file) and with the four document folders the administrator selects. It saves uploaded student documents into those folders, opens them for signed-in staff, and moves them to the trash when staff remove them. It does not read, list or change any other files in the Google account.

The tracker's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Where information is stored

Student records are stored in a Supabase database with access limited to signed-in staff. Documents are stored in the school's Google Drive folders. Information is not sold, shared with advertisers, or used for any purpose other than running the school.

Removing access

An administrator can disconnect Google Drive at any time from the Google Account's third-party access page. Students who want their records corrected or removed can contact the school.
