Idylwood Care Center: clinical requirements

Idylwood has its own requirements. If you choose Idylwood, submit all of these during the first week of the program:

  • Nurse Registration form or demographic information: cbhi.formstack.com/forms/cbhi_intern_application
  • COVID-19 vaccination
  • PPD / chest X-ray or QuantiFERON test results
  • Physical examination
  • CPR
  • Flu vaccination
  • Fingerprinting
  • Sexual harassment training certificate: dfeh.ca.gov/shpt
  • Negative COVID test within the previous 72 hours
  • School agreement and BVNPT or BRN approval
